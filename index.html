Digital Fuel Level Indicator System

Measuring a fuel tank and showing the result as clear digital steps, using a comparator, logic gates and a 7-segment display.

About the project

A simple, low-cost way to turn a physical quantity into digital information.

The problem

Traditional gauges can be inaccurate because of sensor errors, fuel movement and poor calibration, and a needle is hard to read exactly.

Our solution

A sensor signal is compared against fixed reference voltages. Logic gates then drive a 7-segment display showing 0 to 4, plus a low-fuel warning LED.

Where it is used

Cars, bikes, generators and fuel storage tanks, and a great way to learn digital system design.

How it works

From fuel height to a number on the display in six stages.

SensorFloat / potentiometer
→
Voltage dividerResistance to voltage
→
LM339Compares with 4 references
→
Logic gatesXOR · AND · NOT → BCD
→
CD4511BCD to 7-segment
→
Display + LEDLevel 0 to 4, low warning

Low-fuel warning

The red LED turns on when all comparator outputs are 0, i.e. the tank is at the lowest level, giving the driver an early alert.

Results and interpretation

The potentiometer was varied to simulate fuel levels and the outputs were recorded.

✓ Five levels detected

Empty, 25%, 50%, 75% and Full were all shown correctly.

✓ Gates worked

Comparator outputs were converted into the correct BCD signals.

✓ Warning works

The low-fuel LED lit at the minimum level.

Advantages

  • Easy to read and low cost
  • Simple design, low power
  • Low-fuel warning included
  • Works for vehicles, generators and tanks

Limitations

  • Only five levels shown
  • Needs calibration and stable 5 V
  • Fuel movement can cause fluctuation
  • Prototype uses a potentiometer, not a real tank

Conclusion and future scope

The system was designed, built and tested successfully, showing digital design applied to a real problem.
