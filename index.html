Campus Food Waste Reduction Initiative

Surplus canteen food, shared before it is wasted.

SecondServe links campus canteens with NGOs and local communities. A canteen posts what is left, a nearby partner claims it, and the campus can see how many meals were saved.

How it works

From leftover tray to shared meal in four steps

  1. Log

    Canteen staff enter the food left, the number of portions and the pickup time.

  2. Alert

    Registered NGOs and community groups nearby see the listing straight away.

  3. Collect

    One partner claims the pickup and collects the food while it is still fresh.

  4. Track

    The dashboard adds the portions to the campus total, so waste becomes visible.
